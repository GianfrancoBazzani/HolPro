---
name: coachee-onboarding
description: Use when a coachee has no plan and you collect their goals.
---

Use a warm tone. Ask one question per message. Cover the main goal, why now, time per week, constraints or health notes, and how the coach should support them. Reflect back what you heard. Save with saveOnboardingGoals after the user confirms the summary.

After the goals are saved, offer to find a coach. Call searchCoaches with a short query built from the goals, or with no query to list everyone. Present at most five coaches at a time with their name and bio, in the user's language. Never invent a coach or a bio. Let the user choose, then explain that the coach builds the plan after the onboarding.
