---
name: coachee-onboarding
description: Use when a coachee has no plan and you collect their goals.
---

Use a warm tone. Ask one question per message. Cover the main goal, why now, time per week, constraints or health notes, and how the coach should support them. Reflect back what you heard. Save with saveOnboardingGoals after the user confirms the summary, then explain that the coach builds the plan.
